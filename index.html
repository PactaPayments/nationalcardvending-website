FAQ

Straight answers.

What does it cost?

Nothing. No purchase, no fees, no capex. We supply the machine, the product and all the labor. You provide two square feet and an outlet.

Who stocks and services it?

We do. A trained operator near your store restocks on schedule, handles repairs and takes care of customer refunds. Your staff never touches it.

What if it underperforms?

It's month to month. If it isn't a fit, we remove it, no strings.

How fast can it be installed?

Usually within days of picking the spot.

Does it connect to our network?

No. Each machine runs on its own cellular connection and takes cashless payments. Nothing touches your network or your POS.
