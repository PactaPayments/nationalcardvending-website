FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

What does it cost me?

Nothing. No purchase, no rental fee, no maintenance cost. We provide the machine, the product, and all the labor. You provide a bit of floor space and an outlet.

What's in it for my business?

A fun, modern amenity at zero cost and zero effort, plus the extra foot traffic collectors bring — plus a share of sales for hosting.

Do I have to manage or restock it?

No. We handle stocking, restocking, repairs, payments, and customer refunds. Your only job is giving it a spot to live.

How much space does it need?

Very little. The machine has a compact footprint and just needs a visible spot near a standard power outlet.

What if it breaks or a customer has an issue?

Machines are remotely monitored and we respond fast — we handle refunds and maintain the hardware so it's never your problem.

What if it doesn't work out?

Hosting is month-to-month. If it's not a fit, we remove the machine. No strings.
