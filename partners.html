For buyers

Questions we hear from buyers.

Who services the machines?

We do. A trained operator near each store installs, restocks on schedule, repairs and handles customer refunds. Store staff never touch it.

What if a store underperforms?

Every placement is month to month. We can move the machine to a better spot or remove it at any time, no strings.

How fast do installs happen?

Pilot stores are typically installed within days of choosing them.

What do our stores need to provide?

Two square feet of floor space and a standard 110V outlet. That's it.

Does it touch our network or POS?

No. Each machine has its own cellular connection and takes cashless payments on its own.

How do we see results?

Through the partner portal: live sales by store, stock and uptime, plus one monthly statement for every location.
